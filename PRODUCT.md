# FORMA

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Django templates, Tailwind CSS and htmx.

## Users

Persian-speaking shoppers and renovation planners choosing kitchen, bath and building products, including visitors who want to inspect finishes in Rasht.

## Product Purpose

Help shoppers browse and compare products, check finishes and specifications, and coordinate a purchase, quotation or showroom visit.

## Brand Commitments

Use the FORMA identity and a Persian RTL layout. The neighboring prototype includes two desktop/mobile reference boards and matching brand media. The supplied requirements sheet defines the storefront scope.

## Operating Context

Staff edit the catalog and review requests in Django admin. The existing scenario supplies a showroom address and phone numbers. Product, project and finish data carried over from the prototype are development fixtures. Payment, live inventory, confirmed social and map links, and notification delivery are open dependencies.
